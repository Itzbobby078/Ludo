Quick rules
Roll a 6 to release a token from its base. Reach the end of your home lane with an exact roll. Landing on an opponent's unprotected token captures it. Safe squares protect tokens from capture. Roll a 6 or capture a token to earn another turn. The five-player option is a custom variant.

How to play
1. Tap the dice to roll.
2. When the dice shows 6, choose a token to leave your base.
3. Tap a highlighted token to move it.
4. If more than one token can move, choose the one you want.
5. First player to bring all four tokens home wins.
