03 / How to play

Enter the track: Roll a 6 to bring a token out of its base.
Move: Roll and select one of your highlighted tokens.
Capture: Land on an opponent on an ordinary square to send their token home.
Safe squares: Tokens on marked safe squares cannot be captured.
Win: Bring all four of your tokens to the finish.

Rules & reminders

Each player has four tokens. A six grants an extra turn. You must roll the exact number needed to finish. If more than one token can move, choose the token you want on the board. A finished token cannot move again.
