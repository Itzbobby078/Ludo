How to play ♡

Classic Ludo rules for your game night.

  1. Each player has four tokens in their coloured home base.
  2. Roll a six to bring a token onto the starting square.
  3. Move a token forward by the number shown on the dice.
  4. Land on an opponent's token on a capturable square to send it home.
  5. Safe squares protect tokens from capture.
  6. You need an exact roll to reach the final home space.
  7. The first player to bring all four tokens home wins.
The game engine will enforce the actual move rules. Online play needs a connected server and is not enabled by this page alone.
