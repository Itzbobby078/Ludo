How to play

♡ Roll a 6 to bring a token out of its home.
♡ Tap a highlighted token to move it after rolling.
♡ Capture an opponent by landing on their token on an unprotected square.
♡ Safe squares protect tokens from being captured.
♡ Reach the finish with all four tokens to win. You need an exact roll to finish.
