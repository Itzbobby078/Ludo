How to play Ludo ♡

A quick guide before your first roll.

01 · Roll a six
In this version, a six brings a piece out of its base.
02 · Move around the board
Move a piece forward by the number shown on the dice.
03 · Capture and stay safe
Landing on an opponent's unprotected piece sends it back to base. Marked safe squares protect pieces from capture.
04 · Reach home
Your piece needs an exact roll to reach the finish. Get all four pieces home to win.
05 · Roll a six again
A six grants another roll in this version.
