How to Play

Goal

Bring all four of your tokens around the board and into the center. The first player to do it wins.

Rolling

On your turn, tap the big dice. The dice stays locked while you must choose a token.

Leaving the base

You need a 6 to move a token out of its base onto your colored start square.

Moving

A token moves exactly the number rolled. If more than one token can move, glowing tokens are your choices — tap one. If no token can legally move, the turn passes automatically (even after a 6).

Captures

Landing on a square holding opponent tokens sends all of them back to their bases. You do not capture on safe squares, in home lanes, or your own tokens. There are no blockades: tokens may always pass each other, and your own tokens may share a square.

Safe squares

The 8 squares marked with a star are safe: the four colored start squares and the four star squares eight steps ahead of each start. Nobody can be captured there.

Home lane

After almost a full lap (51 squares from your start), your token turns into your own colored home lane. Only your tokens enter it, and tokens inside cannot be captured.

Exact roll to finish

The center needs an exact count. If the roll is too high, that token cannot move. A finished token never moves again.

Extra turns

  • Rolling a 6 gives another roll.
  • Capturing an opponent gives another roll.
  • Bringing a token to the center gives another roll.
  • Three 6s in a row cancel the turn: the third 6 is not played and the turn passes.

Winning

You win when all four of your tokens have reached the center. The game ends immediately.
