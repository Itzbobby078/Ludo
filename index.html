Quick guide

How to play

Roll a six to bring a piece out of base. Move along the board path, capture opponents on eligible squares and guide all four pieces into your home lane. You must roll the exact number to finish.

Safe squares: Pieces on designated safe squares cannot be captured.

Extra turn: Rolling a six gives another roll. If you have no legal move, your turn passes unless you rolled a six.

Fairness: Dice will use browser randomness. Online dice and moves will require server-side validation.
